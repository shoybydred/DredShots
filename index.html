My Story

I'm Kayden, a passionate photographer and videographer specializing in sports and automotive photography. What started as a hobby and just taking photos for family and friends has evolved into a professional career capturing moments that matter.

Even with 1+ year of experience, I've worked with professional racing teams, athletes, and car enthusiasts to create stunning visual content that tells their stories. I am trained by the best in the field and now can keep up with my two feet.

My goal is simple: create photos that aren't just for show, but for memories and your own exposure. Every shoot is an opportunity to capture something special.
